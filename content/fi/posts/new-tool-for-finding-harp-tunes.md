### Find, Convert, and Play: The Ultimate Tool for Harmonica Players! 🎶

### **A New Tool for Playing Folk Music on Harmonica**

Have your ever wondered where you could find folk music sheet music with harmonica tablature or found the perfect folk tune but struggled to figure out how to play it on your harmonica? I’ve developed a specialized tool designed to take the guesswork out of the process.

With this application, you can search thousands of tunes directly from **TheSession.org** and **Folkwiki.se** and instantly convert them into clear sheet music with harmonica tablature.

**Key Features:**

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>**Automatic Tablature:** Enter a tune name, and the tool generates blow and draw indicators directly under the notes.</li>

<li>**Smart Key Recommendations:** The app analyzes the melody and suggests the best harmonica key. It automatically handles octave ranges (C, D, E, F vs. G) for optimal playability.</li>

<li>**"No-Bends" Filter:** Filter your search to show only tunes that do not require bending techniques – a game-changer for clean, natural notes.</li>

<li>**Play &amp; Save:** Listen to the tune in your browser, adjust the tempo, and save your favorites with the heart icon for future practice.</li>
</ul>
