<div class="harmonica-tool-container" style="line-height:1.6;color:#333;max-width:800px;margin:auto;font-family:sans-serif"> ### Find, Convert, and Play: The Ultimate Tool for Harmonica Players! 

Have you ever found the perfect folk tune but struggled to figure out how to play it on your harmonica? I’ve developed a specialized tool designed to take the guesswork out of the process.

 

With [this application](https://janneojajarvi.github.io/harptunefinder/), you can search thousands of tunes directly from **TheSession.org** and **Folkwiki** and instantly convert them into clear sheet music with harmonica tablature.

 ### Key Features: <ul style="list-style-type:none;padding-left:0"> <li style="margin-bottom:10px">**🎵 Automatic Tablature:** Blow and draw indicators directly under the notes.</li> <li style="margin-bottom:10px">**💡 Smart Key Recommendations:** Suggestions for the best harmonica key.</li> <li style="margin-bottom:10px">**🔄 Automatic Transposition:** Optimal octave range (C, D, E, F vs G) handled automatically.</li> <li style="margin-bottom:10px">**🚫 "No-Bends" Filter:** Perfect for clean, natural notes.</li> <li style="margin-bottom:10px">**🔊 Audio Playback &amp; 📄 PDF Export:** Master the rhythm and practice offline.</li> </ul> 

Try it here: [https://janneojajarvi.github.io/harptunefinder/](https://janneojajarvi.github.io/harptunefinder/)

<br>**Instructions / How to Use**

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>**Language: **Switch between languages using the flag icons.</li>

<li>S**earch:** Use the free search field or pre-defined buttons (e.g., <em>Polska</em>).</li>

<li>**Random Tune:** Click the **Random** button to shuffle.</li>

<li>**No-Bends Filter:** Choose to show only tunes playable without bending techniques.</li>

<li>**Display Mode:** Switch between Dark and Light mode using the **Moon/Sun** icons.</li>

<li>**Audio &amp; Print:** Use **Play** to listen and **Print** for PDF export.</li>

<li>**Tempo:** Adjust playback speed with the slider.</li>

<li>**Favorites:** Save tunes with the **Heart** icon and manage them in the menu.</li>

<li>**Settings:** Manually adjust the **Harmonica Key** or **Octave Range**.</li>
</ul>
