### **Interactive Harmonica Bending & Overbend Map**

**Master the entire range of your 10-hole diatonic harmonica with our real-time interactive tool.**

The diatonic harmonica is no longer "just" a blues instrument limited to a single key. By using **bends** and **overbends** (overblows and overdraws), you can unlock all the missing chromatic notes, turning your harp into a fully chromatic powerhouse. *(Note: C, D, E, F and G harmonicas reflect lower octave ranges properly).*

### **How to Use This Tool:**

<ol start="1" style="line-height:1.6;">
  <li><strong>Select Your Key:</strong> Use the dropdown menu to choose the key of your harmonica (C, G, A, D, or Bb). The entire map and the musical staff will update instantly.</li>
  <li><strong>Explore the Notes:</strong>
    <ul style="list-style-type: disc; margin-top: 5px;">
      <li><strong>Blue Buttons (+):</strong> Standard Blow notes.</li>
      <li><strong>Orange Buttons (-):</strong> Standard Draw notes.</li>
      <li><strong>Green Buttons ('):</strong> Draw Bends (holes 1–6) and Blow Bends (holes 8–10).</li>
      <li><strong>Pink Buttons (o):</strong> Overblows and Overdraws – the advanced techniques for chromatic playing.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li><strong>Visual Feedback:</strong> Click or tap any note to hear its pitch and see its exact position on the <strong>treble clef staff</strong>.</li>
</ol>

Whether you are practicing your first draw bend on the 4th hole or mapping out complex jazz scales using overblows, this map provides a clear visual and auditory reference to guide your playing.

If the tool looks too small you can click it bigger from [this link](https://codepen.io/Janne-Ojaj-rvi/full/emdWPVz).
