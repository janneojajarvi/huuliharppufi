### **Interactive Harmonica Bending &amp; Overbend Map**

**Master the entire range of your 10-hole diatonic harmonica with our real-time interactive tool.**

The diatonic harmonica is no longer "just" a blues instrument limited to a single key. By using **bends** and **overbends** (overblows and overdraws), you can unlock all the missing chromatic notes, turning your harp into a fully chromatic powerhouse.

### **How to Use This Tool:**

<ol start="1" class="wp-block-list">
<li>**Select Your Key:** Use the dropdown menu to choose the key of your harmonica (C, G, A, D, or Bb). The entire map and the musical staff will update instantly.</li>

<li>**Explore the Notes:** * **Blue Buttons (+):** Standard Blow notes.
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>**Orange Buttons (-):** Standard Draw notes.</li>

<li>**Green Buttons ('):** Draw Bends (holes 1–6) and Blow Bends (holes 8–10).</li>

<li>**Pink Buttons (o):** Overblows and Overdraws – the advanced techniques for chromatic playing.</li>
</ul>
</li>

<li>**Visual Feedback:** Click or tap any note to hear its pitch and see its exact position on the **treble clef staff**.</li>
</ol>

Whether you are practicing your first draw bend on the 4th hole or mapping out complex jazz scales using overblows, this map provides a clear visual and auditory reference to guide your playing.
