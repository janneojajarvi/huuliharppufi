**ONLINE LESSONS**

Janne Ojajärvi offers **harmonica**, **willow flute** (**overtone flute**) and (**throat singing**) lessons. <span class="tlid-translation translation" lang="en"><span class="" title="">The lessons can be held in Helsinki or remotely</span></span> by Skype or similar programs like Zoom and Facetime. You only need your own instrument, computer with camera+microphone (e.g. laptop) and internet connection. Janne Ojajärvi offers also lessons for jaw harp (jew's harp/mouth harp) and throat singing.

Lessons will be customised according to the needs and interests of the student.

**Harmonica**

Music styles: blues, traditional Nordic folk, rock, film music, folk/country etc.

Topics:<br>Technics, tunes, theory, comping, improvisation, performing, maintenance, tuning, configuring for over bending, microphones, effects, etc.

**Overtone flute / Willow flute** (sälgflöjt, seljefløyte, pikkvile, pitkähuilu)

Music styles: Traditional Finnish, Estonian, Swedish or Norwegian tunes, composed tunes.

Topics:<br>Hearing overtones, technics, exercises, tunes, breath technics, maintenance, buying new instrument, microphones,etc.

**Throat singing**

Topics:<br>Breathing, hearing overtones, producing voice with false vocal chords, technics, exercises, tunes, kargyraa, höömei, sygyt, overtone singing, etc.
