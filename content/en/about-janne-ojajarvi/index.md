Janne Ojajärvi is Finnish artist, music teacher, composer, lyricist, award-winning improviser and harmonica &amp; willow flute player and versatile multi-instrumentalist. Janne has specialized in harmonicas, overtone flutes (willow flute) and human voice. Janne performs as a soloist and with ensembles ([Hurja Halla](https://www.hurjahalla.com/) and [Ritva Nero](https://ritvanero.fi/)).
