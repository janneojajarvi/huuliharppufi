  <p>Have you ever found the perfect folk tune but struggled to figure out how to play it on your harmonica? I’ve developed a specialized tool designed to take the guesswork out of the process.</p> 
  <p>With <a href="https://janneojajarvi.github.io/harptunefinder/">this application</a>, you can search thousands of tunes directly from <strong>TheSession.org</strong> and <strong>Folkwiki</strong> and instantly convert them into clear sheet music with harmonica tablature.</p> 
  
  <h3 style="color:#2c3e50;margin-top:25px">Key Features:</h3> 
  <ul style="list-style-type:none;padding-left:0"> 
    <li style="margin-bottom:10px"><strong>🎵 Automatic Tablature:</strong> Blow and draw indicators directly under the notes.</li> 
    <li style="margin-bottom:10px"><strong>💡 Smart Key Recommendations:</strong> Suggestions for the best harmonica key.</li> 
    <li style="margin-bottom:10px"><strong>🔄 Automatic Transposition:</strong> Optimal octave range (C, D, E, F vs G) handled automatically. *(Note: C, D, E, F and G harmonicas reflect lower octave ranges properly).*</li> 
    <li style="margin-bottom:10px"><strong>🚫 "No-Bends" Filter:</strong> Perfect for clean, natural notes.</li> 
    <li style="margin-bottom:10px"><strong>🔊 Audio Playback &amp; 📄 PDF Export:</strong> Master the rhythm and practice offline.</li> 
  </ul> 
  
  <p>Try it here: <a target="_blank" rel="noopener" href="https://janneojajarvi.github.io/harptunefinder/">https://janneojajarvi.github.io/harptunefinder/</a></p>

<h3 style="color:#2c3e50;margin-top:30px;">Instructions / How to Use</h3>

<ul style="line-height:1.6;">
  <li><strong>Language:</strong> Switch between languages using the flag icons.</li>
  <li><strong>Search:</strong> Use the free search field or pre-defined buttons (e.g., <em>Polska</em>).</li>
  <li><strong>Random Tune:</strong> Click the <strong>Random</strong> button to shuffle.</li>
  <li><strong>No-Bends Filter:</strong> Choose to show only tunes playable without bending techniques.</li>
  <li><strong>Display Mode:</strong> Switch between Dark and Light mode using the <strong>Moon/Sun</strong> icons.</li>
  <li><strong>Audio &amp; Print:</strong> Use <strong>Play</strong> to listen and <strong>Print</strong> for PDF export.</li>
  <li><strong>Tempo:</strong> Adjust playback speed with the slider.</li>
  <li><strong>Favorites:</strong> Save tunes with the <strong>Heart</strong> icon and manage them in the menu.</li>
  <li><strong>Settings:</strong> Manually adjust the <strong>Harmonica Key</strong> or <strong>Octave Range</strong>.</li>
</ul>
